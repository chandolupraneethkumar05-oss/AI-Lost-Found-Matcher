Collection Protocol

How Lost Property is Handled & Reclaimed

A secure four-step procedure ensuring every item reaches its rightful owner.

1

Secure Intake

Items are logged with photographs, assigned a tracking reference, and locked in security locker vaults.

2

Visual Cross-Matching

Photographs are visually analyzed for color, silhouette, brand logos, and specific hallmarks.

3

Proof of Ownership

Claimants answer non-public questions (such as serial numbers, scratches, or interior contents) before release.

4

Verified Handover

Owners present photo identification at the campus security desk to complete physical handover.
